How to play

Setting up

  1. Choose how many players there are, from 2 to 8, and type their names.
  2. Choose an admin password. Only the scorekeeper should know it. It is needed to correct a prophecy after it locks (see Fixing a mistake). Pick something you will remember, because it cannot be recovered.
  3. Tap Deal me in. One person keeps score on their phone for the whole game.

Playing a round

  1. There are 8 rounds. Each player is dealt 8 cards in the first round, 7 in the second, and so on down to 1 card in the last round. The cards number above each column shows this, and NOW marks the round you are on.
  2. Every player prophesies how many tricks they will win. Type it in the top box of their cell. A prophecy cannot be more than the cards dealt, and the page will tell you if it is.
  3. When everyone has prophesied, the prophecies lock and the Bids row shows the total of all the prophecies against the cards dealt: over, under or even.
  4. Play the round. Then type each player's actual tricks in the bottom box. The running total appears on the right of each cell, with that round's points beneath it.

Scoring

  • Exact prophecy: 10 points for every trick prophesied. Prophesy 3 and win 3 and you score +30. Prophesy 0 and win 0 and you score 0.
  • Wrong prophecy: minus 10 points for every trick you were out by, whether too high or too low.

Fixing a mistake

  • A wrong actual can simply be retyped. Actuals stay open once all the prophecies are in.
  • A wrong prophecy is locked, so it needs the admin password. Press and hold the locked box for a second, or tap the small Locked button under the round's number, then enter the admin password. The round opens for editing.
  • Tap Re-lock under the round's number when you have finished. The same hold-and-password works on the greyed-out actual boxes if you need to enter an actual before everyone has prophesied.
  • A quick tap on a locked box only shows a reminder, so you will not open the password box by accident.

The end of the game

  • When the last round is complete there are fireworks, the winner is announced, and the game is saved to history automatically with its date.
  • Tap Share results to send a picture of the scorecard to WhatsApp. On a phone it opens your share sheet, where you choose WhatsApp and the chat or group.
  • Tap New game to start again. It warns you first if the finished game has not been saved.

History

  • Tap History to see every saved game, newest first. Tap a game to open its full scorecard, share it or delete it.
  • History is shared. Anyone with this page's link sees the same list and can add or delete games, so keep the link among friends.

Good to know

  • The current game is remembered on your device, so you can close the page and carry on later.
  • Only one person needs to enter scores. Everyone else can watch over their shoulder or follow along from the shared results picture.
